Design Story and Inspiration for the Sedge Stitch Blanket

I love the sedge stitch! It’s so fun and only uses single crochet, half double crochet and double crochet to create an interesting texture that is a simple one row repeat.

I decided to use it for my next gradient blanket because it’s easy enough for a beginner but relaxing enough for nights on the couch binge watching. Or, in my case, in the car line at school! Haha!

Just like most of my blanket patterns, it comes in 11 sizes from lovey to king so you can make it in almost any size you need. Just choose one, and get going.

Sedge Stitch Blanket

What You’ll Need

Yarn: Lion Brand Mandala Gradient (90% Acrylic/10% Nylon, 295yds/150g) in Dark Teal (See chart below for number of skeins) 

Hook: US Size 7.00mm

Notions: Yarn needle, scissors

Gauge: 13 sts and 6 rows = 4″ in sedge stitch pattern

Approximate Finished Sizes:

Find the video tutorial here!

Stitch Abbreviations
Chain (ch)
Slip Stitch (slip st)
Single Crochet (sc)
Half Double Crochet (hdc)
Double Crochet (dc)
Yarn Over (yo)

Pattern Notes: Round one border stitch counts are estimates based on this pattern’s gauge and the length of the sides of each blanket. As long as a blanket edge has a multiple of 3+2 between each corner ch-2 space, it will work out evenly.

If your border round one stitch count is off and you end with a different number of sts between the last cluster and the ch-2 space, it is okay to sc2tog at the beginning of a cluster to help even out the counts.

Use the numbers for each side of the border as a guide.

Sedge Stitch Blanket Pattern

Or chain any multiple of 3+2 for a custom size.

Row 1: (Sc, hdc, dc) in 2nd ch from hook, skip 2 ch, *(sc, hdc, dc) in next ch, skip 2 ch; repeat from * to last 3 ch, sc in last ch – you should have one less st than your starting ch.

Row 2: Ch 1, (sc, hdc, dc) in first sc, *skip dc and hdc, (sc, hdc, dc) in next sc; rep from * to last 3 sts, sc in last sc; turn.

Repeat row 2 until the following number of rows are complete (including the first 2 rows already worked). 

Border 

Round 1: Ch 1, sc number of sts from chart across top of blanket, (sc, ch 2, sc) in corner, sc number of sts from chart across side of blanket, (sc, ch 2, sc) in corner, sc number of sts from chart across bottom of blanket, (sc, ch 2, sc) in corner, sc number of sts from chart across side of blanket, (sc, ch 2) in same st as first sc, join with slip st to first st; turn.

Round 2: Slip st into ch-2 space, ch 1, (sc, hdc, dc) in ch-2 space, *skip 2 sts, (sc, hdc, dc) in next st; repeat from * around, working (sc, hdc, dc) in each corner ch-2 space. Join to first st with a slip st.

Fasten off.

FINISHING

Weave in all ends.
