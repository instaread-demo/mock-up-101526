Legal challenges mount against Stella Passage development

Port of Tauranga has announced that it is facing legal challenges to the resource consent for the Stella Passage development.

Three appeals and two judicial review applications have been filed against the decision to approve the development, which was issued on September 7.

Ngāi Te Rangi Settlement Trust, Ngāti Pūkenga, and Ngāti Ranginui Iwi Society Incorporated, Ngā Hapū o Ngāti Ranginui Settlement Trust, and the Ngāti Ranginui Fisheries Trust lodged the appeals.

Ngāti Kuku Hapū Trust, Ngā Tai ki Mauao Manatōpū, and Ngā Hapū o Ngā Moutere Trust submitted applications for judicial review.

Port of Tauranga said it intends to vigorously defend the approval and will be seeking an expedited hearing.

The company said it will ask the High Court for a single hearing, as the Fast-track Approvals Act 2024 provides that appeals and judicial reviews relating to the same matter should be heard together.

The Stella Passage development involves extending the Sulphur Point container berth and the Mount Maunganui wharves, along with associated reclamation and dredging.
