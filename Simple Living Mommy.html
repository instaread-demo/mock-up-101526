Halloween is such a fun excuse to make treats you probably wouldn’t bother with any other time of year. My kids love anything that looks creepy, spooky, or just a little ridiculous, so Halloween desserts are always a hit in our house.

I rounded up 15 Halloween desserts that would be perfect for a Halloween party, a fun night at home with the kids, or even just something festive to make during October. Some are spooky, some are cute, and most importantly, these are actually doable. You don’t need to be amazing at decorating cakes or spend half the day in the kitchen to make something your kids will think is awesome.

If you need an easy Halloween dessert this year, there are some really fun ideas here!

Easy Halloween Cut Out Cookies

These Easy Halloween Cut Out Cookies are such a fun Halloween dessert, especially if you have kids who love helping in the kitchen. Grab your favorite spooky cookie cutters, let everyone help decorate, and you have an easy treat that would be perfect for a Halloween party or a fun afternoon at home.

Halloween Candy Corn Mini Cheesecakes

These Halloween Candy Corn Mini Cheesecakes are such a cute addition to a Halloween dessert table. They have those classic candy corn colors without requiring a ton of complicated decorating, and the individual size makes them perfect for parties. If you want something a little different from the usual Halloween cookies and cupcakes, these are a fun one to try.

Halloween Sugar Cookie Bars

These Halloween Sugar Cookie Bars are perfect when you want all the fun of Halloween cookies without having to decorate a bunch of individual cookies. They’re colorful, festive, and easy to cut into squares for a Halloween party or an after-school treat. Plus, anything covered in Halloween sprinkles is pretty much guaranteed to be a hit with the kids.

Easy Halloween Bark

This Easy Halloween Bark is one of those Halloween desserts that looks like you put way more effort into it than you actually did. It’s colorful, fun, and perfect for breaking into pieces and setting out at a Halloween party. This would also be such a fun one to make with the kids since there’s really no need for it to look perfect.

Spider Web Chocolate Pretzels

These Spider Web Chocolate Pretzels are such a cute idea for Halloween, and I love that they’re a little different from the usual cookies and cupcakes. They look impressive enough to put out for a party, but they’re still simple enough to make with the kids. The combination of sweet and salty makes these even harder to resist!

Halloween Eyeball Oreo Truffles

These Halloween Eyeball Oreo Truffles are creepy enough for Halloween without being so scary that the younger kids won’t touch them. They’re such a fun addition to a Halloween party dessert table, and I love how much the eyeball design makes them stand out. These are definitely the kind of treat my kids would get a kick out of!

Oreo Mummy Pops

These Oreo Mummy Pops are almost too cute to eat. They’re such a fun Halloween treat for kids, and the little mummy faces make them perfect for a party or Halloween movie night at home. I especially love Halloween desserts like this that look adorable without requiring you to be an expert at decorating.

Halloween Sugar Cookies with Cream Cheese Frosting

These Halloween Sour Cream Sugar Cookies are such a fun way to turn a classic sugar cookie into something festive for October. They’re soft, colorful, and perfect for letting the kids help with the decorating. Make a batch for a Halloween party, bring them to a school event, or just make them because you need an excuse to eat Halloween cookies!

Halloween Jello Cauldrons

These Halloween Jello Cauldrons are such a fun change from all the cookies and cupcakes that usually show up at Halloween parties. The little cauldrons are so cute, and kids will love the spooky, colorful Jello inside. This is one of those easy Halloween desserts that looks extra special without being a ton of work.

Halloween Ghost Meringues

These Halloween Ghost Meringues are seriously adorable and would look so cute on a Halloween dessert table. Each little ghost has its own personality, which makes them especially fun for kids. They’re a great option when you want a Halloween dessert that feels festive and a little different from everything else you’ve already made this October.

Five Minute Pumpkin Spice Fudge (No Bake, 4 Ingredients)

This Five Minute Pumpkin Spice Fudge is perfect when you need a Halloween dessert at the last minute and don’t have time for anything complicated. It has all those cozy fall flavors we start craving as soon as October rolls around, and you can have it made in just a few minutes. This would be an easy addition to a Halloween party or a fun treat to keep at home for yourself!

Baked Halloween Donuts

These Baked Halloween Donuts are such a fun excuse to have donuts for dessert. They’re festive, colorful, and would be perfect for a Halloween party or a special October breakfast with the kids. I love that they’re baked instead of fried, which makes homemade donuts feel a lot more doable on a busy day.

Halloween Graveyard Pudding Cups

These Halloween Graveyard Pudding Cups are exactly the kind of Halloween dessert my kids would love. They’re spooky and fun without being too creepy, and serving them in individual cups makes them especially great for parties. I love anything that looks this cute but is still simple enough to pull together without spending hours in the kitchen.

Spider Peanut Butter Cookies

These Spider Peanut Butter Cookies are such a cute way to make regular cookies feel a little more special for Halloween. The little chocolate spiders are just creepy enough to make the kids laugh without being over the top. These would be perfect for a Halloween party, or you could make a batch for a fun after-school surprise in October.

The Best Monster Eye Pretzels (Just 3 Ingredients!)


These Monster Eye Pretzels are such an easy way to add something fun and spooky to your Halloween dessert table. They’re colorful, a little creepy, and have that sweet and salty combination that makes it way too easy to eat more than you planned. This is also a great Halloween treat to let the kids help make!

I hope you found at least a few Halloween desserts here that you can’t wait to try! Whether you’re planning a big Halloween party or just want to surprise the kids with something fun after school, you really don’t have to make anything complicated to make the day feel special.

Pick your favorite, get the kids in the kitchen with you, and don’t worry if yours doesn’t turn out quite as Pinterest-perfect as the picture. I promise the kids won’t care. Sometimes the messiest treats end up being the ones they remember the most anyway.

Happy Halloween!
