This ham and cabbage soup is a comforting, cozy meal that's packed with savory flavor, tender vegetables, and perfect for using up leftover ham on chilly days.

If you're looking for a simple, hearty weeknight dinner that will warm you up from the inside out, this cabbage & ham soup is exactly what you need. It’s light yet filling, loaded with tender cabbage, smoky ham, and soft baby potatoes, and comes together effortlessly in one pot.

I love making a big batch of this soup after the holidays when we have leftover ham in the fridge, or during busy weeknights when I need a warm, quick meal on the table. My family loves soaking up the flavorful broth with a slice of warm, crusty bread!

Why You'll Love This Cabbage & Ham Soup

This classic ham and cabbage soup is a weeknight staple for a reason:

  • Quick & easy -- ready in under 35 minutes in just one pot

  • Budget-friendly -- turns simple ingredients into a rich, comforting meal

  • Great for leftovers -- the ultimate way to use up holiday ham

  • Great for meal prep -- tastes even better the next day as the flavors meld together

  • Perfect for sharing -- the ultimate meal to pack in a mason jar and drop off for a sick neighbor or friend in need of a little comfort

If you've been searching for a nourishing, easy dinner recipe, this cabbage & ham soup checks every box!

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced

  • 2 to 3 celery stalks, sliced

  • 2 medium carrots, sliced

  • 2 cups cooked, boneless ham, diced

  • 4 cups green cabbage, chopped

  • 12 oz baby potatoes, diced

  • 6 to 7 cups chicken broth (adjust depending on how brothy you want it)

  • 1 teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)

  • ½ teaspoon black pepper

  • ½ teaspoon dried thyme

  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder

  • 1 bay leaf

Helpful Ingredient Tip

I typically buy ham from Sam's Club when we're feeding the family or saving leftovers for soup prep. If you happen to have a leftover ham bone on hand, toss it right into the pot while the soup simmers—it adds an incredible depth of rich, smoky flavor to the broth!

How to Make Cabbage & Ham Soup

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the diced onion, sliced celery, and carrots. Cook for 5 to 6 minutes until the vegetables are slightly softened.
  2. Stir in the diced cooked ham and cook for 2 minutes to let the flavors warm through.
  3. Add the chopped cabbage, baby potatoes, chicken broth, salt, black pepper, dried thyme, garlic powder, and bay leaf.
  4. Turn the heat to high and bring the soup to a boil. Reduce to a gentle simmer, cover with a lid, and cook for 20 to 25 minutes, or until the potatoes and carrots are tender.
  5. Remove the bay leaf. Taste and adjust seasonings as needed, adding more broth or water if you prefer a brothier soup. Ladle into bowls and serve warm!

Creamy Ham and Cabbage Soup

Want a richer, comforting twist on this recipe? This variation is just as easy.

Stir in ½ cup of heavy cream or half-and-half during the last 5 minutes of simmering. The result is a silky, velvety broth with a subtle creaminess -- perfect for soup lovers looking for extra comfort on a cold evening.

Easy Variations and Add-Ins

This cabbage & ham soup is incredibly versatile. Try mixing in:

  • White beans or cannellini beans for added fiber and texture

  • Diced fire-roasted tomatoes for a savory, subtle tang

  • A splash of apple cider vinegar right before serving to brighten up the flavors

  • A pinch of red pepper flakes if you like a little kick of heat

You can customize it based on what you have in your pantry or your family's favorites.

Storage Tips

Store leftover ham and cabbage soup in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. Reheat individual portions on the stove or in the microwave until warmed through. You can also freeze this soup for up to 3 months.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Can I make this ham and cabbage soup in a Slow Cooker or Crockpot?

A: Yes! Sauté the onions, celery, carrots, and ham as directed in steps 1 and 2, then transfer everything to the slow cooker bowl along with the remaining ingredients. Cover and cook on LOW for 7 to 8 hours or HIGH for 4 to 5 hours until the potatoes and carrots are tender.

Q: Can I use red cabbage instead of green cabbage?

A: While you can technically use red cabbage, it will bleed a purple hue into the broth and turn the soup a dark color. Stick with standard green cabbage or Savoy cabbage for the best taste and presentation!

Q: Is this soup low carb and keto friendly?

A: Because this version includes baby potatoes, it contains some carbs. If you're following a strict keto or low-carb diet, simply swap the potatoes for cauliflower florets or extra cabbage!
