Cinnamon Palmiers are easy to make with puff pastry! Sprinkled with cinnamon sugar and baked to flaky perfection, these “elephant ears” make any morning better.

Have you ever had an “elephant ear” pastry? Cinnamon Palmiers or “Elephant Ears” are a simple treat made of puff pastry rolled into a spiral with cinnamon sugar and baked. The nickname comes from the shape of the palmier: this food really DOES resemble the jumbo ear of an elephant!

SAVE THIS EASY CINNAMON PALMIERS RECIPE TO YOUR FAVORITE PINTEREST BOARD!

This cinnamon sugar palmier recipe is one of the quickest and easiest pastry recipes you can make. The flaky layers of baked pastry dough and sweet cinnamon spice are perfect for pairing with a cup of coffee or tea.

Everyone loves homemade cinnamon sugar pastries for breakfast!

Ingredients for Cinnamon Palmiers

Get this: you only need three ingredients to make these cinnamon sugar palmiers.

  • Puff pastry dough- I use the sheets of frozen store-bought puff pastry and let them thaw in the fridge. So simple and convenient!
  • Sugar
  • Cinnamon

Equipment Needed

  • Rolling Pin
  • Plastic Wrap
  • Small bowl
  • Baking sheet with parchment paper
  • Sharp knife
  • Wire cooling rack

How to Make Cinnamon Palmiers Using Puff Pastry

This recipe comes together in a few easy steps.

Prepare: I preheat the oven to 400 degrees F and prepare a cookie sheet by lining it with parchment paper. Then I mix the cinnamon and sugar together in a small bowl.

Roll out the pastry dough: I spread about half of the cinnamon sugar mix on the counter first and gently unroll the dough into a rectangle on top.

Sprinkle cinnamon sugar on top: I sprinkle the cinnamon and sugar all the way to the edges to ensure every bite is full of delicious cinnamon flavor.

Roll and chill: Now I roll one of the longer sides in toward the center of the rectangle, stopping in the center of the dough. I repeat this with the opposite side, so I have two spiral logs. Then I wrap the rolled-up dough in plastic wrap and chill it in the fridge for about 1 hour.

Bake: I cut the chilled dough into ½ inch slices and place them on the prepared baking sheet about 1-2 inches apart, then bake at 400°F for 12 – 15 minutes. I rotate the baking sheet halfway through the baking time so the cinnamon palmiers bake evenly.

Your kitchen will smell amazing as these are baking! And as good as the palmiers smell, I promise they taste even better.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this recipe with homemade puff pastry dough?

I have never made my own puff pastry dough from scratch for this particular recipe. If you have a go-to recipe you love, I’m sure it would work well here. Give it a try and let me know!

How do I store Cinnamon Palmiers?

Store these baked and cooled cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for 3-5 days. Don’t put them in the fridge, as the moisture inside will dissolve the cinnamon-sugar coating, making the cookies soft and sticky.

Can I freeze these cookies?

Yes, you can freeze already baked Cinnamon Palmiers in a freezer-safe container with parchment paper in between layers. Freeze for 1-2 months, then re-crisp them for a few minutes in a hot oven.

Can I freeze the dough for Palmiers?

Yes, you can roll and chill the puff pastry log, slice it into individual cookies, then freeze them flat on a baking sheet for 30-45 minutes. Once firm, transfer them to a freezer bag and store for 2-3 months. Bake right from frozen, though you might need to add a minute or two to the bake time.

Cinnamon Palmiers


Course Breakfast
Cuisine American
Prep Time 15 minutes minutes
Cook Time 14 minutes minutes
Total Time 1 hour hour 29 minutes minutes
Print
Pin
Rate
Save Saved!
24 slices
Cinnamon Palmiers are easy to make with puff pastry! Sprinkled with cinnamon sugar and baked to flaky perfection, these “elephant ears” make any morning better.

Equipment

  • Rolling Pin
  • parchment paper
  • Baking Sheet

Ingredients
  

  • 1 sheet puff pastry thawed
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 teaspoons cinnamon

Instructions

  • Preheat oven to 400 degrees F.
  • Prepare a cookie sheet by lining it with parchment paper.
  • In a small bowl, mix sugar and cinnamon together.
  • On a clean surface, sprinkle half of the cinnamon/sugar mixture.
  • Gently unroll the pastry sheet over the sugar.
  • Sprinkle the remaining sugar mixture entirely over the dough.
  • With a rolling pin, roll the dough out so that it is about 1/2-1 inch longer on all sides so that it is approximately 10 X 12-inches.
  • Beginning with one of the longer sides, gently roll it towards the middle, stopping in the center of the dough.
  • Do the same with the other side, rolling into the center.
  • Gently press the two rolls together.
  • Wrap in plastic wrap, place on a cookie sheet and refrigerate for an hour until firm.
  • Remove from refrigerator and using a sharp knife, cut dough into 1/2 inch-thick slices and place on prepared cookie sheet about 1-2 inches apart.
  • Bake for 6 minutes, rotate the cookie sheet and then bake for another 6-8 minutes until golden.
