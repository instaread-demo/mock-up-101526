    Big Weekend Ahead For Galway's Basketball Clubs

    Sunday Sport
    Share this article
    Listen to audio version of this article
    Sponsored by
    0:00 / 1:36
    Instaread

    The season may be only two games old but for Galway’s Clubs in the Basketball Ireland League there is a chance to make a real statement this weekend.

    League leaders Maree are at home to Bowes Auto Centre Portlaoise Panthers at Calasanctius College on Saturday evening as they bid to make it three from three while Maigh Cuilinn, who are second, could find themselves closing on the leaders if they beat Ulster University at the Kingfisher.

    Titans can get their first win if they beat Tipp Talons away from home.

    In the Women’s Division One, University of Galway Mystics can also get their first win of the season when they welcome Clunetech Kilkenny Stars to the Kingfisher on Sunday afternoon.
